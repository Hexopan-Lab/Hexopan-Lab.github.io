Who we are

A lab with a simple idea.

Hexopan Lab is an independent software lab. We build desktop and mobile apps for people who care how their tools work: engineers, tinkerers, and anyone who'd rather own their software than rent it.

We believe the best software runs on your device, keeps your data with you, and shows its work. So we write it in the open, where anyone can read the code, file an issue, or send a fix.

How we build

Four things we don't compromise on.

Open source, all of it

Every line we ship is public. Read it, build it yourself, fork it, or help us make it better.

Local-first

Your files, keys and conversations stay on your device. No account needed, and nothing leaves unless you send it.

Private by default

No tracking, no telemetry you didn't ask for, and secrets kept in your system's keychain where they belong.

Nothing hidden

Our tools show what's really happening under the hood: real measurements, readable logs, clear choices.

Open source
